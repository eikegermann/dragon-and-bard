# 6. Follow the Literacy Hub phonics progression

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** November 2026 (recorded 2026-11-22)

## Context

Systematic phonics is well supported by evidence, but no single order of sounds has been proven best. Players go to different schools with different programmes, some are homeschooled, and Victoria now requires systematic synthetic phonics and a Year 1 Phonics Check.

## Decision

All reading content follows the Australian Government's Literacy Hub progression (24 phases, plus a listening phase 0), documented with word bags in the Phonics Ladder doc. Australian English and Australian spelling throughout.

## Consequences

Compatible with Australian schools and homeschooling without depending on one commercial programme. Word lists must be checked against the Literacy Hub's own lists. No module reads the ladder yet; making them do so is the foundation for Story and Explore modes (ADR 0016).
