# 16. Three ways to play: Story, Explore and Practice

- **Status:** Accepted (not yet built)
- **Date:** November 2026 (recorded 2026-11-22)

## Context

Following a phonics sequence strictly conflicts with an open map, where a child can walk into a region whose words are too hard. Parents also want to practise one thing on purpose.

## Decision

Three modes using the same modules. **Story:** a linear route, one exit per region, with regions following the word-bag clusters of the Phonics Ladder doc (about two phases per region). **Explore:** the open map; the next phase comes when the current one is practised enough, and spaced review brings earlier phases back. **Practice:** the sandbox, choosing module and phase.

## Consequences

Modules must take their words from the ladder by phase (ADR 0006). Story mode needs a fixed route and probably its own map layout. Maths has its own ladder; the working suggestion is that reading sets the story route and maths adapts within each region. Discussed in #31.
